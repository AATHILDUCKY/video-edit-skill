## Typography and graphics

Manrope, weights 500 and 800, is bundled locally and embedded in `assets/codex/fonts.css`. Its SIL Open Font License is included in `assets/codex/manrope-OFL.txt`. [Manrope on Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Manrope).

The terminal-style mark is an original graphic for this template, not a reproduction of an official Codex logo. Code, camera, image, scissors, waveform, play, sliders, download, and check graphics are drawn directly as vector paths. The Codex name identifies the tool used in the supplied story.

## Sound and motion

The impacts, whooshes, cut snaps, pings, export confirmation, rhythm bed, and motion were created for this template. The prompt typing uses an actual recording: [IBM M2 sound.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:IBM_M2_sound.ogg), recorded by Anonimski and released under CC0. It is trimmed, faded, and level-adjusted to the prompt's typing window; its pitch is unchanged. The original is stored as `assets/codex/keyboard-source.ogg`.

Separate effects, rhythm, and keyboard stems are bundled as embedded AAC for direct-file browser playback and as WAV for video regeneration. The keyboard has an independent on/off option and follows the sound-effects volume control.
