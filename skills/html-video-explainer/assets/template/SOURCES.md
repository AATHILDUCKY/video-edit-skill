# Assets and credits

All cybersecurity, pentesting, browser, prompt-to-build, open-source toolbox, editor, device, circuit board, task-flow, 3D printer, and follow illustrations are original Canvas/SVG-style drawings in `assets/codex/concept.js`. They are simplified visual references, not product screenshots, precise circuit diagrams, or scan results. No stock photography or external CDN is used.

The Arduino drawing is a stylized circuit board; Arduino is mentioned as the topic of the creator’s projects. No official logo is used. The preview contains a clearly labelled camera slot, not another person’s footage.

Manrope: The Manrope Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Bundled license: `assets/codex/manrope-OFL.txt`. Recorded keyboard source and attribution: `assets/codex/keyboard-source.ogg` and `assets/codex/keyboard-credit.txt`. Keyboard playback occurs only during the terminal/code typing windows. Other effects are synthesized by `scripts/make-soundtrack.py`.
